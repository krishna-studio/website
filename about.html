Our story

Krishna Studio was started by Ramkrishna together with his college friends Keerthana, Anjali, Usman and Talha. We spent years building things for classes and for fun, and we wanted a place to do it properly, together.

Right now Ramkrishna and Keerthana are building our website, and the rest of the team is joining as we pick up more projects.
